Privacy Policy

PS Workspace is a local-first office suite. This page explains — in plain language — what stays on your PC, what never reaches us, and what happens when you choose to use AI.

The short version

We collect nothing. Documents stay on your device. AI only works with your own key, sent directly to the provider you choose. You can delete local app data at any time.

01

Data we collect#

We do not collect personal data.

No accounts, telemetry, analytics, advertising, crash uploads or cloud sync. We do not run a server that receives your documents or usage.

The only information that leaves your device is what you deliberately send to a third-party provider you configured (such as an AI prompt or a search query), content fetched by optional features you open (such as the downloadable font catalog), or a message you email to us.

02

Documents & local data#

Your files stay where you put them. Documents you open or create are processed on your device and saved to the location you choose. PS Workspace does not upload them to us or to a cloud service.

Preferences and other app data are stored in the app's local data folder on your PC. You can delete that folder at any time. Documents you created are not deleted unless you delete them yourself.

03

AI features & API keys#

AI is optional. It stays off until you add an API key for a provider you choose. When you use an AI feature, your prompt or selected content is sent directly from your device to that provider using your key — never through a PS Workspace server.

Your API keys stay on your device.

Keys are stored with Windows secure storage (DPAPI through Electron safeStorage) and are never sent to us. You can remove a key at any time in Settings.

04

Search & media providers#

Optional web search and media generation use the provider you configure. Search queries or prompts are sent directly to that provider, under its own policy. If you do not configure these integrations, they are unavailable.

Search providers include Serper, Serply, Tavily, Parallel, Keenable, Exa and Firecrawl. Media generation uses the image or video provider you enable.

05

Local integrations & logs#

PS Workspace can run a local MCP bridge bound to 127.0.0.1 so local tools can talk to the app. It is reachable only from your own computer and is never exposed to the internet.

Diagnostic logs for this bridge are stored locally in the app data folder and are not uploaded. The app has no remote backend and no self-updater; Microsoft Store updates are delivered by the Store.

06

Telemetry & analytics#

None. The app includes no third-party tracking, analytics, advertising or fingerprinting code. This page loads no third-party scripts either. We do not sell or share personal information.

07

Support & contact form#

For questions, privacy requests or security reports, email pratyush60069@outlook.com.

The contact form on ps-workspace.online is processed by Formspree so the message reaches our inbox. Formspree receives the name, email address and message you submit; we use them only to reply.

The contact button on this page is different: it opens your own email app and sends nothing to a server from this page.

08

Your choices#

  • Use the app entirely offline.
  • Remove any AI key at any time in Settings.
  • Delete the app's local data folder whenever you want.
  • Ask us to delete support emails you sent to us.
  • No account to close — there isn't one.
09

Children#

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

10

Security#

Your data is protected by your Windows account and device security. API keys are sealed with OS-level encryption, and documents stay on your device, so there is no central store of your files to breach. Keep your device account and keys private.

11

Changes to this policy#

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will make it clear at the top of this page.
